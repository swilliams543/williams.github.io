How to Get Started as a Writer

Posted November 25, 2025

I have loved to write since I was a child. You may also love to write and know that you want to be a writer or novelist. That dedicated interest is an important first step: without it, you may find it harder to see a writing project through to the end.

Tip 1: Dedication and Motivation. A writer needs consistency and motivation to carry a project from start to finish. I am also my own editor, so I edit my stories after I write them.

The second step is to start writing. When I first began, I set aside an hour each evening to work toward finishing my first novel. You may not need that same schedule as you develop your craft, but a steady routine can help build discipline and momentum. Following mine, I finished the novel in about a year.

Join me for the next post, where I’ll share more tips for growing as a writer. Happy writing!
