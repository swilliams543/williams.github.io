Writer’s Block

Also known as a Pause for Inspiration

Do you ever reach a stopping point in your writing? You’ve been writing this interesting novella, and all of a sudden the plot grows slow and you are not inspired as to where to take your story and you put your foot on the brakes as your beloved project comes to a screeching halt.

You don’t know how to build the momentum or add in a plot twist of some sort. Then you may do like many writers and stop writing. Some may abandon the writing project altogether. Today, I want to share some tips with you that can turn your stopping point into a pause and give you time for inspiration. So instead of calling it a stopping point we can call it a blissful pause for inspiration.

Tips to Turn a Pause into Inspiration:

  • Pray for Guidance: If you have faith like I do, a good tip is to pray that God will give you inspiration. I had reached a pause for inspiration that lasted for months. Finally, I prayed and asked God if He wanted me to continue to work on this project, would He give me inspiration. Less than a few days later, God helped me think of some fresh new directions to take the story and now I’m writing again. Although it’s an old project, I have fresh inspiration from God.
  • Be Kind to Yourself & Take a Vacation: Don't beat yourself up. Give yourself time to breathe and take a break from your story. Go on a trip or go on that vacation that you’ve been planning for a while but keep putting off.     How to Get Started as a Writer

    Posted November 25, 2025

    I have loved to write since I was a child. You may also love to write and know that you want to be a writer or novelist. That dedicated interest is an important first step: without it, you may find it harder to see a writing project through to the end.

    Tip 1: Dedication and Motivation. A writer needs consistency and motivation to carry a project from start to finish. I am also my own editor, so I edit my stories after I write them.

    The second step is to start writing. When I first began, I set aside an hour each evening to work toward finishing my first novel. You may not need that same schedule as you develop your craft, but a steady routine can help build discipline and momentum. Following mine, I finished the novel in about a year.

    Join me for the next post, where I’ll share more tips for growing as a writer. Happy writing!
