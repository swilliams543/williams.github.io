The author

A little about Sarah

Sarah Williams is a Christian fiction and nonfiction writer who lives in North Carolina. She has taught at the NC Pre-K level for more than eleven years. Her second motivational book, Not Rejected, reflects her desire to share what she has learned, give glory to God, and contribute literature that is inspiring and faith-filled.

Latest release

Nubia's Song

Nubia arrives at university hoping to sing as a soloist, make friends, and focus on her studies. When obstacle after obstacle unsettles her plans, she questions whether she chose the right path. Through faith and unexpected encounters, Nubia finds the song God has always wanted her to sing.

Egyptian series · Book one

Egyptian Moon

Hotep, a Nubian prince, travels to Egypt seeking military success, but tragedy leaves him enslaved and without hope. Mandisa, a young woman facing a truth that could shake her foundations, must decide whether to trust the stranger or rely on the old ways. When their worlds collide, both must choose what to believe.

Egyptian series · Book two

Egyptian Dawn

Mandisa's growing feelings for Iuty are interrupted when her aunt's son is abducted and more peasants begin to disappear. When officials fail to organize an adequate search, Mandisa takes matters into her own hands. Will her bold petition to Pharaoh bring justice, or create trouble of its own?

Egyptian series · Book three

Egyptian Sun

As Mandisa explores her newfound faith, a poor decision made on Iuty's behalf drives a wedge between them. Their sudden separation brings uncertainty: will it lead to heartbreak, or become the turning point that guides Iuty toward his destiny and helps the lost be found?

Motivational nonfiction

Not Rejected

For anyone carrying the pain of repeated rejection, this practical book explores how rejection can shape self-image and relationships. It offers ways to recognize those effects and move toward healing through the power of Jesus Christ.

Faith and relationships

7 Myths to Avoid While Waiting for Mr. Right

Waiting for the right relationship can bring doubt and tempting shortcuts. This book explores common myths, including seeking substitutes while waiting, and offers encouragement grounded in God's word for single readers seeking clarity and purpose.
