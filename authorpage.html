Writer’s Block, also known as a Pause for Inspiration

Transform your writing roadblocks into a blissful pause for fresh ideas and renewed creativity.

Inspirational Writing Corner
Author & Creative Encourager

"A stopping point can become the place where inspiration breathes anew."

Do you ever reach a stopping point in your writing? You’ve been writing this interesting novella, and all of a sudden the plot grows slow and you are not inspired as to where to take your story and you put your foot on the brakes as your beloved project comes to a screeching halt.

You don’t know how to build the momentum or add in a plot twist of some sort. Then you may do like many writers and stop writing. Some may abandon the writing project altogether.

"So instead of calling it a stopping point, we can call it a blissful pause for inspiration."

Today, I want to share some tips with you that can turn your stopping point into a pause and give you time for inspiration. So instead of calling it a stopping point we can call it a blissful pause for inspiration.

Key Tips to Reignite Your Writing

1. Turn to Faith & Prayer

If you have faith like I do, a good tip is to pray that God will give you inspiration. I had reached a pause for inspiration and it lasted for months. Finally, I prayed and asked God if he wanted me to continue to work on this project would he give me inspiration—and less than a few days later God helped me think of some fresh new directions to take the story and now I’m writing again. Although it’s an old project, I have fresh inspiration from God.

2. Rest & Change Your Scenery

Another tip is to not beat yourself up. Give yourself time to breathe and take a break from your story. Go on a trip or go on that vacation that you’ve been planning for a while, but keep putting off, and allow yourself to take in the sights and surroundings and this just may be the inspiration that you need for your story. A change of scenery and environment can do that for you.

3. Talk to Family & Friends

My last tip is to talk to your family and friends. Share with them your struggle and ask them where you should pick up where you left off from. They could be of great help to you.

Key Takeaway

Never abandon your dream project when the momentum slows. Reframe your block as a blissful pause—pray for divine guidance, step out for a relaxing change of scenery, and converse with loved ones to find your novel's path forward!

Happy Writing!
